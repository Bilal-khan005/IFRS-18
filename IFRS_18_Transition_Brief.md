# IFRS 18 Transition Brief

2026-11-22 · Bilal

## Why it matters

IFRS 18 replaces IAS 1 for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, so calendar-year reporters adopt it in FY2027 and must restate FY2026 comparatives. Earlier application is permitted. Recognition and measurement do not change; what changes is how performance is presented, labelled and explained. Expect effort in reporting, systems, investor communication and audit, not in the accounting itself.

## Key changes

- **Five P&L categories:** operating, investing, financing, income taxes and discontinued operations. Every income and expense item must sit in one.
- **New mandatory subtotals:** operating profit and profit before financing and income taxes. Operating is the residual category, so classification of investing and financing items drives the result.
- **Management-defined performance measures (MPMs):** subtotals of income and expense that management communicates publicly must be disclosed in a single note, with reconciliation and explanation. They fall within the audit scope.
- **Aggregation and labelling:** stricter rules on grouping and disaggregating line items, and on how "other" categories are described.
- **Knock-on amendments:** IAS 7 (cash flow starting point), IAS 33 (restricted additional EPS measures) and IAS 8, which is renamed Basis of Preparation of Financial Statements.

## Transition requirements

Application is full retrospective, in annual and interim statements. For a December year-end, that means FY2026 comparatives and every 2027 quarter on the new basis, plus a reconciliation of the IAS 1 and IFRS 18 statements of profit or loss for the transition disclosure. Regulators such as ESMA also expect issuers to disclose expected material effects in reports for periods ending before 1 January 2027, so FY2026 year-end reporting is the first test.

## Recommended actions

1. **Gap assessment:** map every P&L line and chart-of-accounts item to the five categories; resolve judgement areas such as FX differences, derivative gains and losses, and mixed-activity items.
2. **Restate FY2026:** build the comparative P&L and the IAS 1 to IFRS 18 reconciliation now, alongside the year-end close.
3. **MPM inventory:** list every subtotal used in earnings releases, investor decks and board packs; keep, retire or reconcile each one.
4. **Systems and controls:** update the ERP mapping, consolidation and reporting tools; document classification policies and controls ahead of audit.
5. **Downstream impacts:** test covenants, bonus and KPI definitions, and segment reporting against the new operating profit.
6. **Communication:** brief investors and rating agencies before the first 2027 interim report; involve IR, legal, IT and audit from the start.
